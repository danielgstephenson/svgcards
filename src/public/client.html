Phase 1 - Play

- Draw from left side of queue until you have 3 in hand, then take pawns if needed
- Exile 1 and play 1 from your hand face down
- Add exile cards to exile stack, reveal and use play cards

Phase 2 - Scandal

- Put leftmost palace card on trial (If Empress leaves the palace, game ends immediately)
- Put highest rank played card on trial, ties go to dungeon
add other played cards to right side of queue

Phase 3 - Auction (all cards on trial)

- Raise your bid by any amount of gold any time (no changing silver into gold)
- May withdraw if tied and not last to bid that amount (withdrawn bids stuck at 0 this round)
- At any time, anyone may ask "Is there no one else?"
- If no one wants to raise or withdraw, highest untied bidder pays bid and takes 1 or more cards
on trial into queue from lowest to highest (if all bids tied trial cards go to dungeon)
- Remaining cards on trial go to dungeon

Victory

- Points equals rank of cards in hand
- 1 point for gold and silver (10 gold = 10 points)
- No points for cards in queue, play, or exile
- Tiebreaker is highest card in hand

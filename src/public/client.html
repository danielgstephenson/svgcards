Phase 1 - Plan

- Exile 1 card from your hand face down behind name tag
- Play 1 card from your hand face down
- Draw from deck until you have at least 3 in hand, then take pawns if needed

Phase 2 - Scandal

- Reveal play cards simultaneously
- If total played eyes more than player count, put next lowest rank palace card on trial

Phase 3 - Powers

- Use listed powers on card you played
- If palace is empty, game ends

Phase 4 - Arrest

- Put lowest rank palace card on trial
- Put highest rank played card on trial, ties go to the dungeon

Phase 5 - Auction

- Announce bonus powers on played cards
- Raise bid by any amount of gold any time (not silver)
- Withdraw if tied and not last bidder
- When no one wants to raise or withdraw, highest untied bidder takes 1 or more cards on trial into discard
- Remaining cards on trial go to dungeon

Phase 6 - Discard

- Discard played card
- Cards in discard enter deck from lowest to highest

Victory

- Points equals rank of cards in hand
- 1 point for gold and silver
- No points for cards in deck, play, or exile
- Tiebreaker is highest card in hand
